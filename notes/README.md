## 📂 Tashkil qilish tartibi

```
notes/
├── TEMPLATE.md              # ushbu shablon nusxasi
├── ctf/
│   ├── 2026-01-15-picoctf-bof1.md
│   └── 2026-02-03-overthewire-narnia4.md
├── machines/
│   ├── htb-easy-linux-machine1.md
│   └── thm-ad-basics.md
└── concepts/
    ├── rop-chains.md
    └── kerberoasting.md
```

- **`ctf/`** — individual CTF masalalari uchun (bitta fayl = bitta masala)
- **`machines/`** — HTB/THM to'liq mashina writeup'lari uchun
- **`concepts/`** — texnika/tushunchani chuqur tushuntirib yozgan konspektlar (masalan "ROP zanjiri qanday ishlaydi")

Fayl nomlash: `YYYY-MM-DD-qisqa-nom.md` (CTF/machines uchun) yoki `mavzu-nomi.md` (concepts uchun) — bu xronologik va qidiruv uchun qulay.
